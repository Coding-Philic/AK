Course Details

Course Code: BPharm101

Duration: 4 Years

Credits: 160

Coordinator: Dr. Emily White

Schedule: Weekdays, 8:00 AM - 2:00 PM

Location: Pharmacy Block, Room 305

Course Description:

The Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) program provides comprehensive knowledge in pharmaceutical sciences, including drug formulation, pharmacology, medicinal chemistry, and clinical practice. The course combines theoretical lectures with practical laboratory sessions and industry internships enabling students to become skilled pharmacists.

Key Topics Covered:

  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry
  • Pharmacology and Toxicology
  • Pharmaceutics and Drug Delivery Systems
  • Clinical Pharmacy and Therapeutics
  • Pharmaceutical Analysis
  • Pharmacognosy and Herbal Medicines
  • Pharmacy Practice and Management

Assessment Methods:

  • Theory Exams: 40%
  • Practical & Lab Work: 30%
  • Projects and Internships: 20%
  • Class Participation: 10%

Required Materials:

  • Pharmacy Textbooks and Lab Manuals
  • Access to Laboratory Equipment & Computers
  • Notebook and Stationery

Learning Outcomes:

Upon completion, students will be able to:

  • Develop comprehensive knowledge of pharmaceutical sciences.
  • Prepare and dispense medications safely and effectively.
  • Conduct pharmaceutical research and quality control.
  • Engage in professional pharmacy practice and patient counseling.

Syllabus

  • Year 1: Basics of Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Human Anatomy, and Physiology
  • Year 2: Pharmacology, Microbiology, and Pharmaceutics
  • Year 3: Clinical Pharmacy, Pharmacognosy, and Pharmaceutical Analysis
  • Year 4: Industrial Pharmacy, Drug Regulatory Affairs, Internship, and Project Work

About B.Pharm

Bachelor of Pharmacy is a professional degree that focuses on the study of drugs, their preparation, effects, and use in medicine. Graduates work as pharmacists, researchers, or specialists in healthcare settings, pharmaceutical industries, and regulatory authorities.
