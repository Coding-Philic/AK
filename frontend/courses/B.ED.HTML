Course Description:

The Bachelor of Education (B.Ed) program prepares students to become competent, reflective, and innovative educators. The curriculum covers pedagogy, educational psychology, curriculum design, and teaching methodologies combined with practical teaching experiences in various educational settings.

Key Topics Covered:

  • Foundations of Education and Philosophy
  • Child and Adolescent Development
  • Teaching Methods and Strategies
  • Curriculum Planning and Evaluation
  • Educational Technology and Media
  • Inclusive Education and Special Needs
  • Classroom Management and Guidance

Assessment Methods:

  • Assignments and Projects: 30%
  • Midterm Exams: 25%
  • Practicum Evaluation: 25%
  • Final Examination: 20%

Required Materials:

  • Textbooks and Reference Materials (details provided)
  • Access to Educational Technology Tools
  • Notebook and Stationery

Learning Outcomes:

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

  • Design and implement effective lesson plans.
  • Understand educational theories and apply them in classrooms.
  • Manage diverse classrooms with inclusive teaching techniques.
  • Utilize modern educational technology for teaching and learning.

Syllabus

  • Year 1 Semester 1: Foundations of Education, Child Development, Teaching Methodologies
  • Year 1 Semester 2: Curriculum Design, Educational Psychology, Assessment Techniques
  • Year 2 Semester 1: Educational Technology, Inclusive Education, Practicum I
  • Year 2 Semester 2: Classroom Management, Guidance and Counseling, Practicum II

About B.Ed

The Bachelor of Education degree is a professional course that equips aspiring teachers with the necessary pedagogical and theoretical skills. It blends academic knowledge with practical teaching experiences and is essential for those who wish to pursue teaching careers at schools and other educational institutions.
