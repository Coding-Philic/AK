Course Details

Course Code: BScN101

Duration: 4 Years

Credits: 160

Coordinator: Dr. Anjali Kumar

Schedule: Weekdays, 8:30 AM - 2:30 PM

Location: Nursing School, Room 204

Course Description:

B.Sc Nursing is a professional degree program designed to prepare students to become competent nurses who can provide quality healthcare services. The curriculum integrates theoretical knowledge with clinical practice, focusing on health promotion, disease prevention, and patient care.

Key Topics Covered:

  • Fundamentals of Nursing
  • Anatomy and Physiology
  • Pharmacology
  • Community Health Nursing
  • Medical-Surgical Nursing
  • Maternal and Child Health Nursing
  • Psychiatric Nursing
  • Nutrition and Dietetics

Assessment Methods:

  • Theory Exams: 40%
  • Clinical Practicum: 30%
  • Assignments and Projects: 20%
  • Attendance and Participation: 10%

Required Materials:

  • Textbooks and Clinical Manuals
  • Access to Healthcare Facilities
  • Notebook and Stationery
  • Uniform and Nursing Equipment

Learning Outcomes:

Graduates will be able to:

  • Deliver comprehensive nursing care to patients.
  • Demonstrate critical thinking and clinical decision-making skills.
  • Promote health, prevent illness, and support rehabilitation.
  • Work collaboratively with healthcare teams and communities.

Syllabus

  • Year 1: Basic Nursing, Human Anatomy, and Physiology
  • Year 2: Pharmacology, Microbiology, Pathology, Medical-Surgical Nursing
  • Year 3: Community Health Nursing, Maternal and Child Health Nursing
  • Year 4: Psychiatric Nursing, Research, Leadership, and Final Clinical Internship

About B.Sc Nursing

B.Sc Nursing is a respected professional course preparing students to meet global healthcare demands. It blends scientific knowledge with compassionate care to empower nurses to serve diverse communities effectively and advance their careers.
