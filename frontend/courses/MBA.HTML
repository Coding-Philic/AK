Course Details

Course Code: MBA101

Duration: 2 Years

Credits: 120

Coordinator: Dr. Robert Lee

Schedule: Weekdays, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM

Location: Business School, Room 420

Course Description:

The MBA program is designed to offer advanced knowledge and skills in business leadership, strategic management, finance, marketing, and entrepreneurship to prepare students for senior management roles.

Key Topics Covered:

  • Strategic Management
  • Financial Management and Accounting
  • Marketing Strategy and Research
  • Operations and Supply Chain Management
  • Organizational Behavior and Leadership
  • Business Ethics and Corporate Governance
  • Project Management
  • Entrepreneurship and Innovation

Assessment Methods:

  • Case Studies and Exams: 50%
  • Group Projects and Presentations: 30%
  • Internships and Field Work: 20%

Required Materials:

  • Core Textbooks and Academic Journals
  • Access to Business Simulation Software
  • Notebook and Stationery

Learning Outcomes:

Graduates will be able to:

  • Lead organizations with strategic vision and ethical responsibility.
  • Use data-driven decision-making and innovative problem-solving skills.
  • Manage financial resources to maximize organizational value.
  • Develop effective marketing and operational strategies.

Syllabus

  • Year 1 Semester 1: Fundamentals of Management, Marketing Principles, Economics
  • Year 1 Semester 2: Financial Accounting, Organizational Behavior, Quantitative Methods
  • Year 2 Semester 1: Strategic Management, Business Ethics, Operations Management
  • Year 2 Semester 2: Entrepreneurship, Project Management, Internship & Dissertation

About MBA

The MBA degree is a globally recognized professional qualification that prepares graduates to take leadership roles in business and management, blending theory with practical skills to impact the global economy.
