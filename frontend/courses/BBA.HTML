Course Details

Course Code: BBA101

Duration: 3 Years

Credits: 140

Coordinator: Dr. Michael Smith

Schedule: Weekdays, 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Location: Business Studies Building, Room 302

Course Description:

The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) prepares students for leadership roles in business through comprehensive study of management, finance, marketing, and entrepreneurship. The program combines theoretical frameworks with practical case studies and internships.

Key Topics Covered:

  • Principles of Management
  • Financial Accounting and Analysis
  • Marketing Management
  • Human Resource Management
  • Business Communication
  • Operations Management
  • Entrepreneurship and Innovation

Assessment Methods:

  • Term Exams: 40%
  • Projects and Presentations: 30%
  • Internship and Practical Work: 20%
  • Class Participation: 10%

Required Materials:

  • Textbooks and Case Study Materials
  • Access to Business Software and Tools
  • Notebook and Stationery

Learning Outcomes:

Graduates will be able to:

  • Apply business theories to real world scenarios.
  • Manage organizational resources efficiently.
  • Develop strategic marketing and financial plans.
  • Innovate and lead in dynamic business environments.

Syllabus

  • Year 1: Fundamentals of Business, Accounting, and Economics
  • Year 2: Marketing, Human Resources, Financial Management
  • Year 3: Strategic Management, Entrepreneurship, Business Ethics, Internship

About BBA

The Bachelor of Business Administration is a globally recognized degree that equips students with the skills and knowledge required for a successful career in business industries, management, and entrepreneurship.
